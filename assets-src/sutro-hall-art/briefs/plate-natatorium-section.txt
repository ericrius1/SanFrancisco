Image brief — a lithographed plate that will hang, framed, on the timber wall of
an indoor swimming hall in a stylised low-poly video game. PORTRAIT 2:3.

Subject: an architectural plate of the baths, in the manner of a period
engineering journal. The upper two thirds is a clean cutaway LONG SECTION through
the glass hall — the arched iron roof trusses drawn as fine line work, the tiers
of galleries, and the seven pools stepped along the floor below, each pool filled
with a flat teal tone. The lower third is a small measured PLAN of the same seven
pools, keyed with roman numerals I to VII. Thin ruled border, a scale bar, fine
hatching for the rock the building sits on. Precise, technical, restrained — the
one plate on this wall that is a drawing rather than a picture. No crowds.

Style: 1890s American lithographed architectural plate. FLAT spot colours over
fine black line work, hard silhouettes, visible flat colour separations, NO
photorealism, NO gradients other than simple flat bands, no digital airbrush.
Slight aged cream paper tone. Much more white/cream space than the poster plates.

Palette (use these, they must sit in a warm timber room): cream #f7eed6, warm
gold #e8ab3f, coral #e07a4e, terracotta #a8412a, deep teal #12454a, mid teal
#1f6b6b, sea green #2e7f6d, ink navy #1b2b45, moss #4d6b3a.

Typography, spelled EXACTLY, in an elegant Didot-like serif, well kerned:
  top small line: PLATE II
  headline: THE NATATORIUM
  subhead: LONGITUDINAL SECTION & PLAN
  bottom rule line: SEVEN BATHS · ONE ROOF OF GLASS
Leave a clean margin — the plate gets a physical frame around it, so keep all
type and key subject matter inside the middle 92% of the canvas.
