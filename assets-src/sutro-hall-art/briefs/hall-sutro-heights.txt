Image brief — a lithographed poster that will hang, framed, on the timber wall of
an indoor swimming hall in a stylised low-poly video game. LANDSCAPE 3:2.

Subject: Sutro Heights, the clifftop pleasure gardens above the baths, at golden
hour. A formal parapet walk along the cliff edge lined with white classical
statues on plinths, clipped hedges, palms and flowerbeds in flat colour blocks,
Victorian visitors in period dress strolling in small dark silhouettes, and
beyond the parapet the whole Pacific in banded teal with a low gold sun. Grand,
ornamental, serene — the counterpoint to the busy interior plates.

Style: 1890s American chromolithograph poster. FLAT spot colours, hard
silhouettes, visible flat colour separations, NO photorealism, NO gradients other
than simple flat bands, no digital airbrush. Slight aged cream paper tone.

Palette (use these, they must sit in a warm timber room): cream #f7eed6, warm
gold #e8ab3f, coral #e07a4e, terracotta #a8412a, deep teal #12454a, mid teal
#1f6b6b, sea green #2e7f6d, ink navy #1b2b45, moss #4d6b3a.

Typography, spelled EXACTLY, in an elegant Didot-like serif, well kerned:
  headline: SUTRO HEIGHTS
  subhead: GARDENS ABOVE THE SEA
  bottom rule line: OPEN TO ALL · FREE TO THE PUBLIC
Leave a clean margin — the poster gets a physical frame around it, so keep all
type and key subject matter inside the middle 92% of the canvas.
