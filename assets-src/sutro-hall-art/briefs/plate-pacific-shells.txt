Image brief — a lithographed plate that will hang, framed, on the timber wall of
an indoor swimming hall in a stylised low-poly video game. PORTRAIT 2:3.

Subject: a natural-history plate of Pacific shore life, as a Victorian museum
would have published it — a companion piece to a botanical plate of ferns. Eight
to ten specimens arranged in a loose ornamental grid on cream stock: an abalone
shell showing its iridescent inner face, a spiralled whelk, a scallop, a sand
dollar, a purple sea urchin, a five-armed ochre starfish, a mussel cluster, a
sprig of bull kelp with its float. Each specimen drawn crisply, isolated, faintly
shadowed, with a small italic numeral beside it. No scene, no horizon, no people.

Style: 1890s American lithographed natural-history plate. FLAT spot colours over
fine line work, hard silhouettes, stipple and hatch shading only, NO photorealism,
NO gradients other than simple flat bands, no digital airbrush. Slight aged cream
paper tone, generous cream space between specimens.

Palette (use these, they must sit in a warm timber room): cream #f7eed6, warm
gold #e8ab3f, coral #e07a4e, terracotta #a8412a, deep teal #12454a, mid teal
#1f6b6b, sea green #2e7f6d, ink navy #1b2b45, moss #4d6b3a, plum #6b2b47.

Typography, spelled EXACTLY, in an elegant Didot-like serif, well kerned:
  top small line: PLATE VII
  headline: SHELLS OF THE PACIFIC SHORE
  bottom rule line: GATHERED AT SEAL ROCKS
Leave a clean margin — the plate gets a physical frame around it, so keep all
type and key subject matter inside the middle 92% of the canvas.
