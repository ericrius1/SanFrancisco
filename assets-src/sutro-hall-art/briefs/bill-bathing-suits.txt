Image brief — a lithographed poster that will hang, framed, on the timber wall of
an indoor swimming hall in a stylised low-poly video game. PORTRAIT 2:3.

Subject: a house bill boasting about the scale of the baths' own laundry and
hire stock. The upper two thirds is a flat decorative arrangement of striped
woollen bathing costumes hung in neat rows on a line, with stacks of folded white
towels below them, drawn as a repeating ornamental pattern rather than a scene —
the pleasure of the plate is the sheer countable quantity of it. Small attendants
in white aprons at the base for scale. Warm, domestic, faintly comic in its pride.

Style: 1890s American chromolithograph house bill. FLAT spot colours, hard
silhouettes, visible flat colour separations, NO photorealism, NO gradients other
than simple flat bands, no digital airbrush. Slight aged cream paper tone.

Palette (use these, they must sit in a warm timber room): cream #f7eed6, warm
gold #e8ab3f, coral #e07a4e, terracotta #a8412a, deep teal #12454a, mid teal
#1f6b6b, sea green #2e7f6d, ink navy #1b2b45, moss #4d6b3a.

Typography, spelled EXACTLY, in an elegant Didot-like serif, well kerned:
  headline: TWENTY THOUSAND
  subhead: BATHING SUITS
  middle line: AND FORTY THOUSAND TOWELS
  bottom rule line: FRESH LAUNDERED DAILY · HIRE INCLUDED
Leave a clean margin — the poster gets a physical frame around it, so keep all
type and key subject matter inside the middle 92% of the canvas.
