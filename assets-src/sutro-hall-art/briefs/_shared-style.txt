
Style (identical across this whole series — they hang side by side on one wall):
1890s American chromolithograph poster. FLAT spot colours, hard silhouettes,
visible flat colour separations, crisp line work, aged cream paper stock. NO
photorealism, NO soft digital airbrush, NO modern gradients — only simple flat
bands and stipple/hatch shading as a stone lithograph would give.

Palette, use these and little else (they must sit in a warm timber room):
cream #f7eed6, warm gold #e8ab3f, coral #e07a4e, terracotta #a8412a,
deep teal #12454a, mid teal #1f6b6b, sea green #2e7f6d, ink navy #1b2b45,
moss green #4d6b3a, plum #6b2b47.

Typography: elegant Didot/Bodoni-like serif, generous letter-spacing, well
kerned, spelled EXACTLY as given and nothing else — no extra words, no signature,
no watermark. Keep all type and key subject matter inside the middle 92% of the
canvas; the poster gets a physical frame around it.
